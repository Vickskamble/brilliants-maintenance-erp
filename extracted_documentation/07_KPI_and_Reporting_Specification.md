# KPI & Reporting Specification

## Maintenance KPIs

### PM Compliance

Completed PM / Planned PM × 100

### Schedule Compliance

Work orders completed within planned period / Total planned work orders
× 100

### MTBF

Operating time / Number of failures

### MTTR

Total repair time / Number of repair events

### Availability

Available time / Planned operating time × 100

### Breakdown Rate

Number of breakdowns per selected equipment/time period.

## Reports

### Asset

-   Equipment register
-   Critical equipment
-   Equipment history
-   Equipment status

### PM

-   PM calendar
-   PM due
-   PM overdue
-   PM compliance
-   PM history

### Breakdown

-   Breakdown register
-   Failure mode analysis
-   Downtime report
-   Equipment-wise failures

### Calibration

-   Due instruments
-   Overdue instruments
-   Calibration history
-   Failed calibration

### Inventory

-   Stock position
-   Critical spares
-   Consumption
-   Stock movement

### Cost

-   Equipment-wise cost
-   Department-wise cost
-   Work-order cost
-   Monthly maintenance cost

### Management MIS

-   Monthly maintenance summary
-   PM performance
-   Breakdown performance
-   Downtime
-   Cost
-   Critical equipment status
-   Pending actions

All KPI definitions must use documented date ranges and filters.
