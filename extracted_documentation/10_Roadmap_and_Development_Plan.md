# Roadmap & Development Plan

## Phase 0 --- Foundation

-   Product architecture
-   Database
-   Authentication
-   Organization/plant
-   Roles/permissions
-   Audit foundation
-   Design system

## Phase 1 --- Core Maintenance ERP

-   Equipment master
-   Equipment hierarchy
-   Critical equipment
-   PM planning
-   PM checklist
-   Work orders
-   Breakdown
-   Corrective maintenance
-   Maintenance history
-   Basic dashboard

## Phase 2 --- Quality & Resource Modules

-   Calibration
-   Inspection
-   Shutdown
-   Spare inventory
-   Vendors
-   AMC/warranty
-   Documents
-   Maintenance cost

## Phase 3 --- Reliability & MIS

-   MTBF
-   MTTR
-   Availability
-   Advanced reports
-   Management MIS
-   Trend analysis
-   Export

## Phase 4 --- Mobile & Field Experience

-   Technician mobile/PWA
-   QR equipment access
-   Offline-friendly forms
-   Photo/document capture

## Phase 5 --- Automation

-   Notifications
-   Automatic schedule generation
-   Approval workflows
-   Recurring work orders
-   Email/WhatsApp integrations

## Phase 6 --- IoT & Predictive

-   Sensor ingestion
-   Condition monitoring
-   Thresholds
-   Anomaly detection
-   Predictive models
-   AI recommendations

## Development Rule

Do not start Phase 5/6 before the Phase 1 data model and transaction
history are stable. Good historical data is the foundation for later
analytics and prediction.
