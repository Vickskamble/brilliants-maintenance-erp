# Brilliants Industrial Maintenance ERP --- Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision

Brilliants Industrial Maintenance ERP is a multi-tenant industrial asset
and maintenance management platform designed to digitize the complete
maintenance department workflow.

The first release is **manual-first**. It does not depend on IoT,
sensors, AI prediction, or automatic machine integration.

## 2. Primary Goals

-   Maintain a complete equipment and asset register.
-   Identify and manage critical equipment.
-   Plan and execute preventive maintenance.
-   Record breakdown and corrective maintenance.
-   Manage calibration and inspection activities.
-   Manage work orders.
-   Maintain spare and maintenance inventory records.
-   Track vendors, AMC, warranty and technical documents.
-   Track maintenance cost, downtime and reliability KPIs.
-   Generate plant and management reports.
-   Provide role-based access and audit history.
-   Keep the architecture ready for future automation, IoT and
    predictive analytics.

## 5. Non-Goals for Initial Release

-   Automatic IoT sensor ingestion
-   Automatic machine control
-   AI-based failure prediction
-   Autonomous work-order generation
-   Direct PLC/SCADA integration

## 6. Success Criteria

The system should allow a plant to replace spreadsheet/register-based
maintenance records with a centralized digital system and produce
reliable maintenance history and management reports.
