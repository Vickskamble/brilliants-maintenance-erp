# Product Positioning

## Product Name

Brilliants Industrial Maintenance ERP

## Category

Industrial Asset, Maintenance & Reliability Management Platform.

## Core Promise

Digitize and manage the complete industrial maintenance lifecycle from
equipment registration and preventive maintenance to breakdowns,
calibration, spares, shutdowns, costs and reliability reporting.

## Initial Positioning

This is not an IoT-first product.

It is a **maintenance-management-first ERP platform** that can later
connect with automation, IoT and predictive analytics.

## Primary Users

-   Manufacturing plants
-   Process industries
-   Engineering industries
-   Utilities
-   Hospitals and large facilities where industrial-style maintenance is
    applicable
-   Multi-site organizations

## Differentiation Direction

-   Modular
-   Multi-plant
-   Practical maintenance workflows
-   Strong equipment history
-   Critical equipment management
-   PM + breakdown + calibration in one system
-   Future-ready for IoT and AI without requiring them initially

## Messaging

**Manage Assets. Plan Maintenance. Control Downtime. Improve
Reliability.**

Avoid unsupported claims such as guaranteed savings or guaranteed
failure prediction.
